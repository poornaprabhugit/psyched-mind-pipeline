**Title:** Why you'd turn down free money 💵
**Description:**
```
In the ultimatum game, one person splits a sum and the other can accept (both get paid) or reject (nobody gets anything). Across 37 papers, people offered about 40% on average, and low offers were often rejected – people give up free money to punish an unfair split.
```

```
Students guessed when they would finish their honors thesis. Average guess: about 34 days. Actual: about 56. Fewer than 1 in 3 finished by their guess, and about half missed even their worst-case date. Psychologists call it the planning fallacy.
```
