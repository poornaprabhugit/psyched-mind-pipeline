**x01** — Your brain remembers the worst moment and the ending, not the whole thing. It's called the peak-end rule. In the study, 69% of people chose to repeat the LONGER cold-water trial. Source: Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber & Redelmeier (1993), Psychological Science. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1993.tb00589.x
