**Question:** Which topic words get the most YouTube searches in our 3 markets, to guide topic picks and long-video titles?
**Method:** Google Trends Explore, *YouTube Search*, past 12 months, US / IN / GB. 4 sets of 5 terms, each with "dopamine" as anchor, so all terms are shown as an index vs dopamine = 100. Raw values 0–3 are too small to trust.

## Related searches for "psychology" (YouTube, US, past year)
- Top: AP Psychology, criminal psychology, Dark Psychology (+20%), The Psychology of Money (book), behavior (+40%), human behavior (+30%), Carl Jung (+20%).
- Rising: consumer behaviour (+250%), marketing (+170%), social media (+200%), trader (+170%), reverse psychology (+70%), dog behavior (+300%).

## What it says (facts)
1. US and UK look alike; India is different: there, motivation dominates and dopamine, overthinking and anxiety come next, while ADHD and narcissist are small.
2. Our label "money psychology" is searched very little in all three; people search concrete words (dopamine, habits, consumer behaviour, The Psychology of Money).
3. Common ground in all three markets: motivation, memory, dopamine, overthinking, anxiety.

## Limits
- Trends is relative interest, not counts, and only search. Most Shorts views come from the feed, so this matters most for **long-video titles and topics**, less for Shorts.
- One 12-month window; no check of seasonality yet.
