# Angle research – which angles work and why

_Started 2026-11-15 (user request: "research which angle works best and why"). The daily run adds 1–3 new outliers to the log; the weekly batch updates the pattern table and uses it for ideas. Never copy content – borrow the pattern, not the video._

**Source quality rule:** many "niche stats" pages (FluxNote, ShortEdge, FlowShorts, RecurPost…) are AI-tool marketing with invented numbers ("500K–5M views per Short"). Trust real view counts on named videos and channel data (OutlierKit, Rosetta, Social Blade-style pages, the platform itself). Mark vendor claims as [vendor].

## Cross-cutting rules (from the above + YouTube/vidIQ)
- The viewer is the subject: "you", their moment, their money – not "researchers found".
- Retention beats raw views: the Shorts feed tests a small audience first and stops if they swipe (vidIQ).
- Niche consistency: Shorts that jump between unrelated topics make it harder for YouTube to find the audience (vidIQ). → Watch whether health & mind Shorts dilute the wealth/everyday audience.
- Money/status angles carry long-form best (Alux, Graham, Johnny Harris are all long). Shorts work best on P1/P2/P6 – instant, visual, personal.

## Hypotheses for the 21 Nov batch (only if the 16–22 Nov data doesn't contradict them)
1. Wealth Shorts framed as P1/P2 ("You feel richer when you pay cash" / "People who check prices twice…") rather than pure study facts.
2. One P2 identity Short on a money topic vs a study-led wealth Short (one variable).
3. Long-video titles built on P3/P4 (rich vs normal, belief reversal).
