# Voice file – how The Psyched Mind sounds

_Created 2026-11-15 (user asked for #6). Read before writing any title, script, caption or description. Add examples as data comes in (date them)._

## Audience and language (playbook §0)
- Viewers: US first, then India, then UK (Canada, EU after). Many are not native English speakers.
- Plain, simple English. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- American spelling: color, center, favor, behavior, leveled, realize, organize. Dollars as "$40" on screen, "forty dollars" in voiceover.
- Talk to "you". Describe a moment the viewer has lived.

## Words we never use
- Slang or idioms a non-native speaker may not know: snap (= get angry), gaslight, rizz, lowkey, no cap, slay, it hits different, mind = blown.
- Hype and clickbait words: insane, shocking, you won't believe, secret they don't want you to know, 100%, guaranteed, proven.
- Expert claims: as a psychologist, doctors say, science proves.
- British-only spellings and words: centre, favour, colour, behaviour, levelled, whilst, mum, queue (use "line").

## Titles
- The question the viewer would type, with "you", plus one emoji at the end. Under ~60 characters.
- Good (our data): "Why you forget why you walked into a room 🚪" – 607 views and 10 likes on day 1, our best Short so far (14 Nov 2026).
- Good pattern: "Why you hear your name in a noisy room 👂", "Why prices end in 9 – and why it works on you 🏷️".
- Avoid: clever wordplay the viewer wouldn't search ("You don't like it. You've just seen it a lot"), study names in titles, slang ("Why you snap after...").

## Hook (first line, first 3 seconds)
- States the study's result or names the moment, in under ~12 words.
- Hook card: 8 words max, a contrast where the study has numbers ("$34 dress vs $39 dress?").
- First clip literally shows what the first line says.

## Script
- 15–25 seconds (under test – top channels run ~55–60 s, see scoreboard).
- No researcher names, universities or years in the voiceover – they go in the description.
- Loop ending: the last line runs straight into the first line.

## Captions (Instagram/Facebook)
- Line 1 = the hook (shows before "more"). Then 2–3 plain sentences with the study's result. "Source: Authors (year)". "Follow for one real psychology study a day 🧠". 3–5 hashtags.

## Money amounts (user decision, 15 Nov 2026)
- Always use **US dollars** in scripts, cards and titles. No rupees or pounds.
- **Never add a "Works the same in rupees/pounds/euros" or "works in any currency" line** – not in the voiceover, captions, descriptions or upload notes (user, 28 Nov 2026).
- If a finding depends on **absolute US income or prices** (e.g. the $75K happiness study, US salaries), say "in the US" and never claim it works the same everywhere.
