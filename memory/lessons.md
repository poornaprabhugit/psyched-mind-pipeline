## 21 Nov 2026 – batch 2026-11-21
- **Root cause of the "tighten didn't run" lessons (14 Nov):** on the runner the old select/aselect tighten cut the VIDEO but left the AUDIO uncut; pad_to_audio then froze the last frame for the same number of seconds ("tightened 1.21 s" → "padded video by 1.21 s"), so burned-in captions ran up to ~1.2 s ahead of the voice by the end. Fixed: tighten now cuts with trim/atrim + concat and raises if video and audio differ by >0.15 s. Always compare the two log lines; equal numbers = desync.
